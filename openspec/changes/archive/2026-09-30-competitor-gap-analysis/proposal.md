# Proposal: Competitor gap analysis

## Why
Research evidence becomes more actionable when owners can compare which problem clusters appear across competitor products and where evidence is concentrated or missing.

## What Changes
- Add a deterministic competitor-by-problem-cluster matrix over existing normalized evidence and cluster assignments.
- Show counts, distinct source items, time window, trend, confidence/coverage and representative source-linked evidence.
- Support a project-level competitor list with stable owner-managed identity and explicit unknown/unmapped evidence.

## Package Boundary and Split Assessment
This is a read-model and comparison capability, independently verifiable using a fixed evidence fixture. It depends on MVP research projects, competitor labels, clusters and evidence; it does not depend on additional adapters because it consumes the common source-neutral model. Additional adapters may increase coverage but have an independent lifecycle. Hosted collaboration is deferred.

| Package | Single outcome | Owner/project and language | Boundary/contract | Depends on | Independent oracle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| evidence-grounded-demand-research-mvp | Evidence-linked research and clusters | Kairion; .NET 10/React TS | Research/evidence model | — | MVP fixture |
| additional-source-adapters | Additional policy-compliant ingestion | Kairion; .NET 10 | `ISourceProvider` | MVP | Adapter contract fixtures |
| competitor-gap-analysis | Comparative evidence matrix | Kairion; .NET 10/React TS | Source-neutral project competitor and cluster read model | MVP | Fixed dataset yields exact matrix |

## BFS Impact Map
- Affected: Kairion query/read model, cluster/evidence aggregation API, dashboard matrix and filters.
- Data: matrix derives from stored source items and cluster assignments; counts are deterministic and never generated by AI.
- Failure/boundary: unmapped competitor, no evidence, low sample, stale items, overlapping competitors and insufficient comparison coverage remain explicit.
- Unchanged: candidate retrieval, AI prompts, source adapters, cluster mutation, ranking claims, auth, hosted services and siblings.
- Shared ownership: no recurring compatible product capability exists; keep semantics in Kairion.

## Capabilities
- `competitor-gaps`: compare evidence-backed problem clusters across owner-defined competitors.

## Non-goals
Automated competitor discovery, competitor scoring, feature scraping, market-share estimates, business validation, or recommendations to build a product.
